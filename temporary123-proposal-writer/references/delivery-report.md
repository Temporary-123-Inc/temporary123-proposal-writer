# Final proposal delivery report

Apply this at [workflow step 11](proposal-workflow.md), after writing and checking the actual proposal. The final chat response must use these four labels, in this order: **Link**, **What to update**, **Readiness**, **Winning chances and improvements**. Keep this report, source-status tables and internal competitiveness analysis outside the buyer-facing Word document.

This report does not replace early questions or authorize silently delivering an incomplete proposal. Resolve required factual/pricing answers before delivery unless the user explicitly requests an incomplete draft. Normal unsigned execution fields are allowed in drafts, but required signatures remain unfinished actions for submission readiness.

## 1. Link

Provide a working HTTPS link to the saved, final editable `.docx` and state **Anyone with the link — Viewer** only after verifying that permission. The user gives standing authorization to create that public viewing link for each generated proposal output; do not ask for the same permission again. This authorization applies to the generated proposal Word file, not source documents, folders, the inventory workbook, tax/bank records, internal notes, skill repositories or other existing proposals. Preserve their permissions.

Save the final local Word file first. Upload it through available authorized storage tools as a stored Word file, preserving `.docx` and its Word MIME type; do not silently replace the deliverable with a native Google Doc, PDF or local-only path. Google Docs/browser authoring may be used, but export and share the actual final Word deliverable. A native copy can be secondary when useful and authorized.

Discover the host's real upload and permission capabilities. For Google Drive, use a raw upload action for the `.docx`, then set the generated file to public link viewing through a supported permission action or the authorized browser's Share settings. Company/domain-only sharing is not public anyone-with-link access. A tool that supports only named users or company sharing cannot satisfy this setting; do not invent arguments, use `anyone_at_company` as a substitute, grant public editing, change parent-folder permissions or bypass an organization restriction.

Read back the actual saved file identity, Word type/version, URL and public-view permission using metadata or the browser Share panel. Check anonymous access when available; a successful signed-in open alone is not proof of public access. Use the URL returned by the service or observed on the verified file, not a guessed URL or a temporary authenticated bearer download link. No recipient emails, invitations, outreach or bid submission are authorized by this step.

If no permitted route can upload or grant/verify public access, provide the actual available Word artifact link and state the exact unfinished sharing action or access limitation under **Link**. Never label that fallback public. Continue the other report sections; a capability limitation must remain visible rather than be reported as success.

## 2. What to update

Audit the exact final Word version after layout is stable. Use the [Word QA workflow](word-workflow.md) and [structural inspector](../scripts/inspect_proposal.py), then inspect/search the matching final rendered PDF/pages to obtain locations. DOCX paragraph indexes and contents entries do not establish page numbers.

Scan body text, native tables, headers/footers, text boxes and content controls for bracketed fields, `TBD`, `TODO`, insert/confirm prompts, blank required prices, unfinished mandatory fields and execution lines. Review the requirements/source record too: an absent required attachment or unresolved material requirement is still an action even without a visible placeholder. Do not create placeholders merely to populate this report, delete required information to hide gaps, or claim a missing attachment is enclosed.

List all actual occurrences in this table:

| Page | Section / exact field | What must be entered or supplied | Needed from / next action |
|---|---|---|---|

Use the final rendered page number, with printed page label too if it differs. List every affected page/location when a field repeats; a repeated header/footer may use a verified page range. Preserve enough exact field text to find it. Explain the specific replacement or action rather than saying only “Confirm.” Distinguish missing information from authorized signature/date actions, buyer clarification and missing attachments. An attachment absent from the document has **Not included** as its location, not an invented page.

If permitted rendering fails, use **Page unverified — [section/field]**, state the verification limitation and reflect it in readiness; do not guess a page. After any edit that can change pagination, repeat the affected page audit before sharing the final version. If no placeholders or unfinished actions remain, explicitly say **None found in the final checked version**.

## 3. Readiness

Give **Submission readiness: N% (N/100)**, using the 1–100 scale requested by the user, followed by **Ready for submission** or **Not ready for submission** and the key blockers. This is a document-completion assessment, separate from winning likelihood and separate from permission to submit.

Use the actual solicitation's requirements/source matrix. Optional make/model, dimension or specification-sheet details introduced by the writer do not become mandatory checks or reduce submission readiness unless the buyer or a material compliance question requires them. Record verified passed, failed, not checked and not applicable checks. Use this consistent internal 100-point rubric, not as a substitute for the buyer's evaluation weights:

| Readiness area | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Mandatory eligibility, submission instructions, forms, acknowledgements and required execution | 25 |
| Technical equipment, service, delivery and performance compliance | 25 |
| Complete required pricing, billing basis and arithmetic | 20 |
| Required company, personnel, reference and supporting evidence | 15 |
| Final Word content, format, pagination, navigation and structural/visual verification | 15 |

Within each area, award its weight in proportion to verified passed applicable checks. Partial work earns credit only for subchecks actually passed; unknown or unverified checks do not count as passed. Omit genuinely inapplicable areas and rescale the remaining weights to 100. Round down to a whole percentage, with a minimum display of 1. State the basis briefly and keep the detailed calculation in working notes.

Use 100% only when all applicable submission checks are verified complete. Any required missing answer, unsigned required certification, absent mandatory attachment, unresolved material deviation or unverified required layout keeps the score below 100 and the status **Not ready for submission**, regardless of a high completion score. Do not waive a blocker because the prose is polished, a price is approved or the file was generated successfully. Normal unsigned draft execution lines must be listed as remaining execution when the buyer requires signing before submission.

Include the existing compact resource-status table here, or a verified link to a separate internal source-status note, preserving the user's resource-tracking workflow within these four main report sections. Report public-link status in **Link**; sharing convenience is not a buyer evaluation criterion unless the solicitation makes it one.

## 4. Winning chances and improvements

Read the actual contract's eligibility, responsiveness and award/evaluation method before assessing competitiveness. Distinguish lowest-priced responsive/responsible awards from scored best-value or tradeoff awards. Use published criteria and weights when present; do not invent them or use the internal readiness weights as award weights.

Assess the current bid against mandatory requirements, equipment/availability evidence, service and delivery credibility, relevant past performance, required participation/qualification evidence, pricing and completeness. Identify any potentially disqualifying issue first. A known fatal unmet requirement can make the bid ineligible as submitted; user selection of a substitute does not establish buyer acceptance.

Use verified official budgets, historical awards, incumbent information or other permitted competitive evidence only when actually available and relevant. Verify current/public facts through authorized sources and cite the exact supporting notice/document/page. A budget is not a competing bid; a price-book rate is not proof of market competitiveness. Never invent competitor counts, competitor prices, market ranks or conversations. Do not contact buyers, competitors or references under drafting authorization.

Give a broad **estimated win-likelihood range** only when a reasoned numerical basis exists, label it a judgment rather than a measured probability, and state confidence and key assumptions. Avoid precise percentages unsupported by data. If the available evidence does not support a number, say **Not reliably quantifiable from available evidence**. Give a reasoned low/moderate/high competitiveness assessment only when the evidence supports that relative judgment; otherwise say **Competitive position not assessable from available evidence** and identify the missing comparison information. Always analyze the documented bid against the actual contract's factors and provide strengths, weaknesses and prioritized improvements even when numerical odds or relative ranking cannot be defended. High document readiness does not imply a high chance of winning.

List the highest-impact improvements in priority order, tied to the actual award method: resolve mandatory defects first, then strengthen the factors the buyer evaluates. For each, state the action, why it matters and what evidence or decision is needed. Pricing changes, new commitments and outreach still require their existing authority; an assessment does not approve a discount or create a capability.

## Final response shape

**Link:** [verified saved Word file URL] — actual public-view status or the specific unfinished sharing action.

**What to update:** The page/location table above, or “None found” after the final audit.

**Readiness:** N% (N/100), ready/not ready, concise basis and blockers, plus the compact resource-status table or its verified internal-note link.

**Winning chances and improvements:** Supported estimate/range or an honest quantitative limitation with qualitative assessment, confidence, actual contract-based reasons and prioritized improvements.
