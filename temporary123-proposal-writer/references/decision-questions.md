# Questions with evidence and recommendations

Use this method whenever a required fact or decision remains unresolved after the [workflow's source checks](proposal-workflow.md). Help the user decide using the inventory, current price book, company records and actual solicitation. Do not return only a list of missing inputs or ask the user to research options the existing resources already supply.

## Prepare the question

For each unresolved issue, show:

1. **Requirement and timing:** What the buyer requires, with the section/page and whether it is needed with the bid, upon request or before service.
2. **What our resources support:** The relevant product, rate, person, role or record and its exact source. Distinguish a supported fact from an unconfirmed project arrangement. Identify any source-access limitation.
3. **Options and recommendation:** The useful supported options, normally two or three when available, and a recommended option with a short reason. Include quantities, fit/deviations and supported proposed values for equipment or commercial choices. Show one candidate if only one is defensible; do not pad the list. Label material unresolved assumptions and unknown costs.
4. **Your decision or evidence needed:** Ask the narrow question that will finish the affected section. Name the recommended option in the question so the user can select it or provide a different direction without reconstructing the research.

Combine related questions into a concise message. Use an options table for product packages and a short paragraph or row for simpler issues. A question tool may collect the answer, but its option labels must identify the actual choices. Record the recommendation, answer and remaining checks in working notes; a recommendation or preselected choice is not approval.

## Use the available resources for each kind of gap

| Unresolved issue | Research and recommendation to include | Ask only for what remains |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment mismatch or multiple products | Query the [inventory](inventory-specifications.md) and [rates](rental-pricing-guide.md); follow [product selection](product-selection.md). Compare actual candidates/packages, quantities, material fit, partial/full values and unresolved compliance or availability. Recommend the best evidenced option provisionally. | Which candidate/package to use, plus specific missing technical evidence or availability. User selection does not establish buyer acceptance. |
| Missing price or commercial decision | Show matching sourced rates, applicable standing policies, calculated extensions and the actual missing components. A planning scenario may show an explicitly unapproved conversion or arrangement when defensible. | Approval of the unresolved scenario or the missing amount/basis. Do not borrow a special rate, visit conversion or renewal concession from another proposal. |
| Signer or key personnel | Read [company contacts and staffing candidates](company-snapshot.md#staffing-candidates). Use supported identity/role facts directly; where assignment is unresolved, recommend the person whose recorded role best matches the work and explain why. | A different signer if needed; actual project assignment/availability, required time allocation, or missing résumé fields. A known name is not a confirmed assignment or a complete officers list. |
| Self-performance or subcontracting | Check operational records for the actual work, location and service cycle. Compare evidenced in-house and approved subcontract paths and their scope/cost implications. Recommend a route only to the extent the records support it. | Which route operations can commit to and any missing provider/scope evidence. Equipment ownership alone does not prove that all servicing and transport will be self-performed. |
| Registration, insurance, background checks or other proof | Locate the relevant record and assess the specific buyer requirement and timing. Recommend reusing an applicable valid record, refreshing an outdated record or obtaining the precise missing proof. | The missing document or operational confirmation. Do not ask the user to choose whether an unsupported certification or check is true. |

If no defensible option or recommendation is supported, say so and recommend the next useful evidence/action rather than inventing a solution. For example, request the exact specification needed to assess a candidate or flag the need for authorized buyer clarification. Do not contact the buyer, a supplier or personnel without the necessary authorization.

## Keep proposal decisions separate from reusable evidence

Use existing library facts and explicitly applicable standing policies automatically. Prior proposals can supply the designated cover-letter style and properly supported past-performance evidence; their product choices, prices, staffing assignments, service commitments and approvals are not decisions for the current task. Do not retrieve another proposal to fill those decisions. The same solicitation number in an archived example does not reactivate its approvals.

Use an explicit choice, quote or delegation already supplied by the user for the current task without asking again, unless a material change creates a new decision. A user may explicitly carry an earlier decision into this task; a worker must not infer that instruction from a historical proposal or packaged worked example.

Continue supported research and drafting while questions are pending, and deliver the substantive Word draft without waiting for every answer. Use a precise descriptive red-highlighted placeholder at each unresolved field; keep an unselected candidate or unapproved estimate explicitly provisional. Keep the options comparison, recommendations and decision questions outside the buyer-facing Word document. Follow the [delivery report](delivery-report.md) to list every remaining field/action, its actual rendered page, required content and responsible source/person, and assess submission readiness. Missing answers affect readiness rather than permission to deliver a draft; they never authorize invented facts, signatures or submission.
