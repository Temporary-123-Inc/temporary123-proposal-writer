# Ordered proposal workflow

Follow these 11 steps for each new proposal. The solicitation controls the response itself; this is the research and drafting sequence. Existing references contain the maintained facts and rules, so use them rather than duplicating a new library here.

Before a step uses facts, read the applicable linked resource content, not just its filename, search snippet or URL. Record the source URL/file ID and version, page/section or catalog row, and what was extracted in working notes. Use the existing supported company facts, matching rates and relevant performance records automatically. If a search is empty, browse the designated folder and relevant child folders directly before declaring information unavailable. Sources remain read-only; keep tax details and other sensitive form data out of the skill and general source table.

Ask consolidated, current-scope questions early when required information remains missing, conflicting or inadequate after the relevant source check. Do not wait for step 9. Do not ask a standard project questionnaire or re-ask known facts. Continue independent work while waiting; before final delivery, obtain required answers or explicit instruction to deliver an incomplete draft. Silence is not approval to guess. Ordinary unsigned execution lines may remain blank.

Maintain the source-status table from the first pass. Show a compact version in chat after the initial opportunity/library source pass and update it at delivery, outside the client Word file. Do not delay the first useful status report until every source has been checked. Step 9 reviews the live table; it does not start it.

## 1. Read opportunity & attachments

Read the user's opportunity URL, the actual solicitation/SOW, pricing schedule, attachments, amendments and Q&A through available web/connector tools or an authorized browser. Use the [source hierarchy and repository index](sources-and-company.md) and [supporting repository](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1tVYaXws6YOkV-k2fik05WEv8xXxl22Q7) when identifying company-side supporting files; they do not replace buyer documents.

Extract the buyer, solicitation/version, scope/site, deadline/time zone, term, quantities, required configuration, service frequency, utilities/access, response order/format, pricing units, evaluation, reference criteria and required forms. Apply amendments to the affected requirements. Check attachment controls when a JavaScript notice hides downloads. Ask for inaccessible required material after permitted retrieval routes; do not treat it as reviewed or fabricate scope.

## 2. Load company & contacts

Read the [company snapshot and source rules](sources-and-company.md), [current company-information document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1d4NQDysuJyMFAvfo16WkH02gJvwH6rrNx_ouYcXqtJ4/edit), [Registration/company folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1pFV2PvbIDjvP8bpJlsNu-YMWspS5sX0B), [capability statement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1nBnaWsYXgLhsLgts4nWNoaURwt6oejNJ/view) and relevant files in the [supporting repository](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1tVYaXws6YOkV-k2fik05WEv8xXxl22Q7). The current company-information document contains tax/payment data; use relevant facts only in the authorized response and do not mirror its contents into the skill.

Extract the legal name, address, contacts, source-supported titles, relevant identifiers/history and company capabilities needed for this response. Reuse ordinary supplied facts without demanding independent live proof. Check freshness where the buyer requires current status, expiry, availability or qualification evidence. A company contact is not automatically the newly assigned project manager.

## 3. Match equipment/specifications

Read the [product-selection process](product-selection.md), applicable equipment descriptions in the [price-book PDF](../assets/rental-price-book-reading-copy.pdf) and [searchable page-indexed text](../assets/rental-price-book.txt), the [catalog](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1tu6szuEWANuI0gtqrsl9g_gQa0Vm256YD7UmsEtouOc/edit?gid=943101906#gid=943101906), and the matching equipment folder linked in its row. Use the [catalog-reading instructions](trailer-images.md) and [provenance record](../assets/source-provenance.json) to interpret these resources. Read buyer specifications from step 1 alongside them.

Compare required versus supported offered family, model/configuration, quantity, fixtures/stalls/beds, capacity, dimensions, accessibility, power/water/waste, footprint and accessories. Record the actual page and catalog row/folder used. A catalog category, image, price or larger model does not establish exact specifications, availability or buyer acceptance. Ask early for required unresolved model details or substitution evidence.

If there is no exact match, show actual supported substitute options and one provisional recommendation; for multiple required units/products, compare repeated-unit or mixed-product packages where useful. Use the linked selection process to quantify coverage, differences and supported proposed values, then ask the human proposal writer/user to choose early unless the current-project selection is already supplied or explicitly delegated. Keep the option table outside the buyer Word file unless required/approved as formal alternates; continue independent work while the selection is pending.

## 4. Plan service/logistics/technical approach

Read the [Technical Approach guidance](proposal-blueprint.md#technical-approach), applicable service/transport/support pages in the [price book](../assets/rental-price-book-reading-copy.pdf), [pricing scope rules](pricing-rules.md), and relevant [company/project evidence](sources-and-company.md). Read the current buyer's site, utility, service and transition requirements again where needed.

Extract the supported deployment sequence, contractor/buyer responsibilities, transport/setup/removal treatment, actual service interval, cleaning/pumping/water/consumables scope, maintenance/escalation and readiness dependencies. Separate an operating plan from an unsupported staffing, local-depot, response-time or inventory commitment. Ask only for missing commitments required by this proposal now; distinguish execution/post-award evidence from bid-stage requirements.

## 5. Apply pricing

Read and apply the [pricing rules](pricing-rules.md), [option/package value rules](product-selection.md#proposed-value-and-billing-basis) when comparing alternatives, applicable pages/qualifications in the [price-book PDF](../assets/rental-price-book-reading-copy.pdf) and [searchable text](../assets/rental-price-book.txt), the buyer's pricing form, and any current user-approved quote/instructions for this project. Use the [provenance record](../assets/source-provenance.json) when distinguishing the bundled reading copy from a newer source.

Populate matching supported rates automatically and calculate quantities/extensions with decimal arithmetic. Record source page/item, billing unit, included scope, qualifying term and project-specific overrides. Resolve required missing billing basis, coverage, conversion or optional-period treatment early. Do not import a historical project's quote as a global default, put zero for an unknown, hide missing costs or silently place a per-visit figure in a monthly field.

Price each defensible candidate on its own supported mix and quantities while a choice is needed; distinguish rental, service and logistics, compatible-period subtotals and unknown components. A provisional recommendation is not the final equipment plan: obtain the user's choice, explicit selection delegation or explicit incomplete-draft direction before final delivery, and preserve any outstanding buyer-acceptance requirement.

Choose examples using the buyer's actual scope, age/count and required fields. Extract supported project/customer, role, deliverables, dates, value basis and reference contact details. Use supplied month/year dates and stated contract values when that precision/basis is adequate. Ask for exact days or final actual costs only if required. Preserve ceiling-versus-earned-value distinctions, source discrepancies and reference-use restrictions; a draft bid is not an award. Do not contact references without authorization.

## 7. Select images when applicable

Read the [image workflow and caption rules](trailer-images.md), relevant rows in the [trailer catalog](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1tu6szuEWANuI0gtqrsl9g_gQa0Vm256YD7UmsEtouOc/edit?gid=943101906#gid=943101906), their actual linked folders, and the [additional approved image folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/183wcGD5Zo-fu5qtz-BaTMhrqxxI-EbWC). Relevant restroom fallbacks are [the source restroom folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1urVsllbGMhX-IyybufdG0bbcDqah--kz), [bundled sink/interior image](../assets/images/restroom-sink-interior.png), [its Drive source](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1r5-6USyN0FBE0vOtHLJoMCIh4BklsQYQ/view), [bundled urinal/interior image](../assets/images/restroom-urinal-interior.png), [its Drive source](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wEwo4WrwxIMkUwS9--yN9ad12T-XIue6/view) and [asset provenance](../assets/source-provenance.json).

Inspect the actual pixels, select only useful equipment-matching views, record row/file and qualification, and prepare accurate captions with preserved aspect ratios. Representative images do not establish offered-unit specifications or compliance. Mark imagery not applicable when it is unnecessary. If suitable optional imagery is unavailable, omit it and note the gap separately; buyer-required images/drawings follow the required-input gate.

## 8. Check required forms/evidence

Read the buyer's actual forms and stage requirements, [Required Documentation guidance](proposal-blueprint.md#required-documentation), [company source guide](sources-and-company.md), [Registration/company folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1pFV2PvbIDjvP8bpJlsNu-YMWspS5sX0B) and relevant files in the [supporting repository](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1tVYaXws6YOkV-k2fik05WEv8xXxl22Q7). The existing [signed W-9 source](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1g-DTUD1oVBKdy7PfbruGl3OXAN-se8fc/view) is available for actual required forms; read/check its details and suitability before reuse, without copying tax data into the skill or general status table.

Determine what is needed with the bid, upon request, at execution or after award. Reuse applicable available forms and ask for actual required missing records after checking folders/versions. Do not sign, invent certifications, preselect unsupported disclosures, say absent attachments are enclosed, delete buyer-required information to hide a gap, or import unrelated tax/bank records.

## 9. Review resource table & resolve questions

Review and update the table already maintained since the initial source pass. Use these exact columns in chat:

| Step | Source actually used (link) | Status | Missing info / next action |
|---|---|---|---|

Use concise rows for material sources/requirements. Link the actual reviewed file/page or local resource, not merely a parent folder when a specific file was used. Record page/section/row and extracted facts in working notes; include a short locator in the table when useful. If no source has yet been used, write `None yet; planned: [resource link]` rather than presenting that link as reviewed.

Resource statuses are **Reviewed**, **Partial**, **Missing**, **Access blocked**, **Not applicable** and **Not yet checked**. Reviewed means the relevant content was actually read for this proposal; it does not imply full proposal completeness or freshness beyond the evidence. Partial means only some needed content was checked or available. Missing means a needed input remains unavailable after the relevant source/folder check. Access blocked means a source is known but unreadable with available authorized access. Not applicable requires a scope-based reason. Not yet checked means it has not been read; it is not a finding of missing information. Split rows or qualify the locator when parts have different statuses.

For authoring and QA steps 10-11, use **Pending** until the document is written or the named checks are performed. Use **Completed** only for work actually performed, listing any narrow verification limitation; use **Partial** when work remains unfinished. Available tools or linked instructions alone do not prove completed authoring or verification.

Do not invent a completed current baseline from the presence of this guide, a stored link or an earlier review. Consolidate only the questions still required by the present scope, avoid duplicates, and identify which answers are needed now versus later. Ask as gaps emerge in earlier steps, then resolve/update them here. Required answers or explicit instruction to deliver an incomplete draft remain necessary before final delivery; no response does not authorize guesses or required blanks. Keep internal completion instructions and this table outside the client Word document.

## 10. Write Word proposal & cover letter

Read the [proposal blueprint](proposal-blueprint.md), [Word authoring workflow](word-workflow.md), [original style/cover-letter reference](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rG5FWpSKjbR6IkqA11jhGdmRs_BpE-Ac4OQ7bB4EYzQ/edit?tab=t.0) and [original USCG Word source](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ySbAfUtMlLk-7sncLW1n_bEjueUh6q3X/view) for structure/voice, not old opportunity facts. For portable creation use [create_proposal.py](../scripts/create_proposal.py) and its `--schema` contract when a suitable native authoring capability is unavailable.

Write substantive project-specific prose, a personalized cover letter in the same editable `.docx`, supported specifications, native pricing tables, relevant performance and required documentation in the buyer's required order/format. Preserve intentionally cited historical performance facts while replacing old title/cover/scope/pricing/buyer-contact details. Keep internal preparation commands separate; ordinary unsigned execution fields may remain. Independent supported drafting can proceed while answers are pending, but final delivery follows the required-input gate.

## 11. Verify & deliver

Read the [verification and delivery instructions](word-workflow.md), [final writing review](proposal-blueprint.md#final-writing-review) and the current resource table. Use [inspect_proposal.py](../scripts/inspect_proposal.py) for structural checks and [render_proposal.py](../scripts/render_proposal.py) with a permitted headless renderer or an authorized current-document PDF export. Inspect every rendered page and correct layout/navigation defects.

Check source-supported claims, scope/configuration, service interval, arithmetic, mandatory forms, resolved required answers, normal unsigned execution lines, cover letter, file integrity and final pagination. If rendering is unavailable, report that narrow verification limitation honestly; never take over desktop apps or claim a visual pass. Deliver the actual Word file and the updated compact source-status table in chat, with only real remaining actions or explicitly authorized incomplete-draft limitations. Do not include the table, source logs or internal completion checklist in the client Word document. No submission, signature, outreach or source-sharing change is authorized.
