# Pricing decisions and calculations

Use the supplied price book and latest user-approved project directions. A source amount becomes a bid rate only after equipment, included scope and billing units match. A buyer's estimated contract value is not the vendor's price, revenue or budget authorization.

Read and apply these rules before drafting the price response. Populate matching supported rates and approved project decisions automatically. After checking the relevant source pages and quote records, ask early for any missing or conflicting price, billing basis, cost coverage or optional-period decision required by this proposal. Do not use a standard pricing questionnaire or ask about items outside its scope. Pending figures may be tracked internally while independent drafting continues; required unanswered pricing needs a user answer or explicit instruction to deliver an incomplete draft, never silence-based approval.

## Preserve billing units and scope

1. Record source page/item, version, price, billing period, exact model/configuration, included service and any qualifying term in working notes. Record user-approved overrides with their project and scope.
2. Calculate with `Decimal` and an intentional two-decimal rounding rule. Show equipment quantities, billing units and extensions. Track incomplete totals in working notes while obtaining the required answer; show them in a delivered document only when the user explicitly requests an incomplete draft. Never hide missing costs.
3. Event rates cover **1–3 days** and are stated as 20% below weekly rates. Use published event amounts. Do not multiply an event amount as though it were a daily rate or invent monthly proration/standard month length.
4. Pages 7–8 portable-toilet/handwash amounts have unconfirmed billing periods and/or service scopes; some are estimates. They do not automatically establish monthly rental with weekly servicing.
5. A three-stall shower/restroom combination does not establish the rate or specifications of a restroom-only three-stall trailer. An accessible portable toilet or differently configured accessible combination does not establish an accessible three-stall luxury trailer. Keep stall counts, layout, toilet/shower functions and accessibility distinct.
6. Page 9's pump-out charges are capacity- and equipment-specific. Do not interpolate arbitrary septic-tank sizes, convert a 300-gallon restroom pump-out into a 350/4,000-gallon rate, or infer emergency dispatch/full cleaning/freshwater refill from pumping alone. Do not double-count the restroom pump-out included in a shower-bladder service.
7. Mobilization ($1,500 per trailer for setup/connections) and demobilization ($1,500 per trailer for disconnection) are separate from transportation. For rentals under three months, delivery and pickup are each $4/mile; over three months, each direction is $1,495 per trailer. Confirm chargeable mileage and the exactly-three-month boundary. A multi-year as-needed agreement does not make each rental continuous for over three months.
8. Add support/staffing only when applicable and sourced. Preserve standard versus weekend/holiday/emergency distinctions; confirm the basis before extending a percentage premium. Preserve the 150-person minimum for man-camp packages and the source's staffing/scope confirmations.
9. Do not assume taxes, deposits, validity dates, cancellation terms, insurance costs, annual escalation, optional-year rates, discounts or minimum orders. New price approval for one project does not change the whole price book.

## Recurring service and conversion

“Every two weeks” means a 14-day cycle. It is not twice monthly. A calendar may contain a different number of visits than a rough annual allowance; use actual dates for quantity estimates when known. If the buyer requires a fixed monthly rate, do not place a per-visit price in that field.

Explain the proposed conversion and its visit-count basis, and obtain a monthly-rate decision when the user has approved only a per-visit amount. Once the user approves a fixed monthly price covering the required cycle, use it without inserting a new visit cap or an unsupported extra-service surcharge. Confirm optional-year rate treatment separately if it is not established.

## Buyer pricing forms and deviations

Use exact mandatory rows, units, periods and order. Include all costs required by the buyer; do not add transport/setup surcharges outside an all-inclusive schedule. Known logistics components can remain internal costing inputs until their treatment is approved. Do not amortize them over an assumed award date or guaranteed rental term.

Follow buyer rules on alternatives, qualifications and exceptions. If company inventory differs, draft the actual offering, flag the mismatch, and identify the required written clarification. Do not change the buyer's stated requirement or claim acceptance because the offered unit is larger. A unit's image or user-approved rental amount does not establish electrical rating, tank capacity, fixtures, new/unused condition or availability.
