Otherwise unspecified stand-alone service charges are per service visit.

Keep source rates, user policy, proposed frequency and calculated totals separate. A per-visit rate times two is a derived monthly subtotal, not a published book monthly rate.
Preserve explicit staffing/day/person, consumables/week/trailer, accessory/month, security/8-hours, mileage/mile and equipment event/weekly/monthly units.
Do not infer unknown rental periods, taxes, fees, discounts, minimum terms, availability, technical compliance or current registration status.
